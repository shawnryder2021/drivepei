# DrivePEI generated image prompts

Both site images were generated with the built-in image generation tool and saved as optimized WebP files. They are editorial illustrations, not photos of a listed vehicle. The homepage now rotates among live vehicles with approved inventory photos; the coastal image remains its fallback when the current feed cannot be verified or no photographed vehicle is active.

## `public/images/pei-coastal-drive.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide homepage hero photo for DrivePEI, an independent Prince Edward Island used-car marketplace. Scene: a modern unbranded charcoal crossover SUV parked at a coastal PEI overlook with red earth shoreline, dunes, grass, calm sea and subtle rolling clouds. Composition: cinematic wide landscape, car on right half, clean darker negative space on left for website headline. Natural late afternoon light, premium editorial automotive photography, believable Canadian Atlantic coast. No people, no logos, no text, no watermark, no dealership signage.

## `public/images/pei-next-drive.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: supporting image for DrivePEI used-car website financing and car finder sections. A candid young adult couple seen from behind walking toward an unbranded used SUV at a scenic Prince Edward Island rural roadside, fields and soft ocean horizon, warm clear morning, authentic Atlantic Canada, editorial lifestyle photography, inviting and inclusive. Wide landscape composition with natural texture. No logos, no text, no watermark, no dealership signage, no visible vehicle badge.
